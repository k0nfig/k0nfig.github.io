From manual step to automated invitation

I built the asynchronous workflow that invited users who didn’t yet have accounts, connecting AWS serverless services with Auth0.

A midnight schedule started the workflow. The sender checked for previous sends, and failures were logged and alerted for recovery on the next daily run.
