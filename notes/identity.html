Identity & access / PowerSchool

One sign-in across products

Centralizing authentication while keeping account context useful across a multi-product environment.

Project write-upAdithya · Lead Software Engineer

On this page

The problemMy contributionInside the systemThe outcome

The problem

PowerSchool’s multi-product environment needed centralized single sign-on that also supported account metadata.

My contribution

As a Senior Software Engineer, I designed and implemented the centralized SSO solution with Auth0 and OpenID Connect, including custom authentication rules for metadata persistence. Later, as Lead Software Engineer, I led development of the MFA service.

Account context alongside sign-on

The implementation included custom Auth0 rules for persisting account metadata. The SSO work therefore covered both authentication and the account context associated with it. MFA was a later service I led across products.

The outcome

60,000+
Daily active users on the SSO platform.
6+ products
Covered by the MFA service I later led.
